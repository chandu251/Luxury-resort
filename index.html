BEACH RESORT IN PATNEM, GOA

The day starts
with the tide.

At six, the beach is still cool underfoot.

Our little stretch of Patnem sits between the Arabian Sea and a stand of old palms. There are 32 rooms, no rush to dress for breakfast, and a path that brings you to the sand before the village wakes.

Serein began with a simple idea: keep the old palms, open the doors to the sea breeze, and let the morning catch decide lunch. The path to the beach is still sandy, the tea still arrives hot, and the day is yours to leave unplanned.
